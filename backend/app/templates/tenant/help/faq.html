With or without VAT?

Every KPI is net. Only the documents' "Grand Total" includes VAT.

Two different "gross profits"

Dashboard: Sales − Purchases (a cash view).
Sales Analytics: Turnover − Cost of goods sold (a commercial view).

Period or snapshot?

Sales, purchases, expenses, cash → period.
Stock, customer/supplier balances → a point in time.

How fresh is the data?

Check the "Last sync" indicator at the top right of every page.
